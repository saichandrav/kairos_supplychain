Catch a medicine shortage
before it ever reaches the shelf.

Some life-saving medicines have a price fixed by law, to keep them affordable. But the cost of making them keeps changing. When it stops being worth it for a manufacturer, they can quietly cut back — and nobody sounds an alarm until the medicine is already hard to find. Kairos is that alarm, ringing months in advance instead of after the fact.
